Selected Research & Computational Projects

Antiterrastrae · Volumetric Biosonar & Scientific Visualization

Ongoing

Self-directed computational prototype · JavaScript, HTML/CSS, WebGL

  • Developing an interactive aquatic simulation connecting three-dimensional animal surface geometry with acoustic propagation, echoes, and temperature-, salinity- and depth-dependent environmental structure.
  • Implemented a tetrahedral finite-element module for scalar acoustic eigenmodes, Kp = λMp, with stiffness and consistent mass matrices, Cholesky transformation and Jacobi eigenvalue iteration.
  • Integrated WebGL echo rendering with a Canvas fallback, adjustable acoustic parameters, and VTK/OBJ export of surface and volumetric geometry for inspection and further analysis.
