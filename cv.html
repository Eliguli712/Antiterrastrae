Cenozoic Chronicle

Evolution × Civilisation · 160 ↔ 160 · English / Deutsch

A double chronology linking 160 evolutionary events with 160 historical events, from the Paleocene to the Miocene. A fixed scale of 10,000 geological years per narrative year supports an order-preserving timeline, civilisation motifs and four comparison curves. The evolution workspace traces 8 comparative sequences, 51 anatomical profiles and 20 calibrated feature axes, with editable inference branches and source-linked evidence.

Selected Research & Computational Projects

Live Orca · Skeletal Anatomy & Measured Movement

Ongoing

Interactive natural-history exhibit · Orcinus orca, MSNUP C–301 · JavaScript, WebGL, Python

  • Preserves all 2,486,130 original scan triangles and provides a 99,982,422-cell surface-derived occupancy field, anatomical region isolation, editable pose and downloadable source geometry.
  • Replays 58,291 calibrated DTAG samples at 50 Hz from Tennessen, Holt & Wright (2024), including measured pitch, roll, heading and depth. Local vertebral motion is identified separately as a reconstruction.
  • Extracts localized visible crown, alveolar-margin and surface-depression candidates from the native scan, transfers shared evidence across display resolutions, and offers learned display stabilization with direct comparison to the original movement record.

Antiterrastrae · Volumetric Biosonar & Scientific Visualization

Ongoing

Self-directed computational prototype · JavaScript, HTML/CSS, WebGL

  • Developing an interactive aquatic simulation connecting three-dimensional animal surface geometry with acoustic propagation, echoes, and temperature-, salinity- and depth-dependent environmental structure.
  • Implemented a tetrahedral finite-element module for scalar acoustic eigenmodes, Kp = λMp, with stiffness and consistent mass matrices, Cholesky transformation and Jacobi eigenvalue iteration.
  • Integrated WebGL echo rendering with a Canvas fallback, adjustable acoustic parameters, and VTK/OBJ export of surface and volumetric geometry for inspection and further analysis.
