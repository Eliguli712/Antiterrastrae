INSIDE THE EXHIBIT

Real bones.
Remarkable detail.

An adult male Orcinus orca, preserved in the collections of the Natural History Museum, University of Pisa.

This mounted skeleton was digitized with an EinScan Pro HD structured-light scanner. Explore the shape of the cranium, the sweep of the ribs, and the articulated vertebral column from any angle.

CollectionUniversity of Pisa
SpecimenMSNUP C–301
AcquisitionStructured-light scan

A surface model records external geometry. It contains no CT intensity values or soft-tissue segmentation. The museum’s scanning workflow includes mesh processing and, where necessary, mounting hardware.

Read the scanning methods

RECONSTRUCTION & MOTION

An anatomy you can move.

Surface-geodesic guides follow the connected scan. The original surface retains every source face and vertex. Optional soft-disc reconstructions interpolate between adjacent endplates without added bulging; they are hidden initially.

Tennessen, Holt & Wright 2024 · calibrated 50 Hz DTAG dataDownload all 58,291 movement samples · float32Movement provenance, channels & dive intervalsBuchholtz & Schur 2004 · delphinid vertebral anatomyFish, Peacock & Rohr 2003 · phased body movements

Cetacean propulsion uses dorsoventral oscillation. Orca swimming kinematics inform the wave controls; regional stiffness follows qualitative evidence from Delphinus delphis. Parameters, cartilage and segmentation are adjustable reconstructions.

Fish 1998 · Orcinus orca kinematicsRohr & Fish 2004 · amplitude and Strouhal numberLong et al. 1997 · vertebral mechanics

Voxel values are interpolated geometric occupancy, not CT density or new measured detail. The grid is rendered as a smoothed, articulated isosurface. Node loads are reduced-order proxies, not a CFD or validated force solution.

Stabilization & anatomy evidence

A fitted quaternion model smooths display orientation. Recording stabilization also handles isolated angular jumps; gentle smoothing keeps smaller corrections. Original angles, acceleration and depth remain available. Synthetic disturbance tests evaluate a display treatment, with no independent clean tracking ground truth.
