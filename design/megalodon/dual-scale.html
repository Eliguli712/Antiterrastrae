Scientific verification boundary. A 33M surface mesh describes geometry and visualization fidelity, not a 33M-degree-of-freedom validated finite-element solution. At a sharp mathematical tip, pointwise stress may remain singular. Convergence must be evaluated at a declared, physically resolved tip radius, across at least three independently refined tetrahedral meshes and with the same dimensional loads and materials.
