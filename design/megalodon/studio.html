Shear & bending method

G = E / [2(1 + ν)]
δb = FL³ / (3EI)
δs = FL / (κGA)

The frame uses spatial Timoshenko beams. Foot translations are constrained, rotations free; no sliding is assumed. Force and moment act at P. Assumed resin density: 1.18 g/cm³; fasteners: 2 g.

ν=0.38, reduction 0.5 and Kt=2 are design assumptions. G describes stiffness. Fracture, pull-out, creep and impact require independent checks.

From coupons to full assembly

  1. Test clearance. Clean and fully cure all six coupons, then fit them to the finished tooth.
  2. Print separately. Slice frame and cap individually; protect negative surfaces, pins and holes.
  3. Insert from the front. Place the root in the rear seat, add clear soft pads and close to the hard stops.
  4. Inspect the print. Check foot coplanarity, paint clearance and retention under a gentle touch before progressive testing.
